# Psi-Doom

## Installation

A release is two files: `psi-doom.pk3` and this readme. The pk3 is the mod folder packed with `MAPINFO`, `KEYCONF`, and `zscript.txt` at the root of the archive.

Psi-Doom needs GZDoom 4.14.2 or newer. It runs with Doom, Doom II, and other maps and mods that leave the original weapons in place. A weapon mod that replaces those guns does not carry these rigs.

Put `psi-doom.pk3` with your other files and load it after the IWAD:

```
gzdoom -iwad DOOM2.WAD -file psi-doom.pk3
```

In a launcher, add the pk3 to the mod list. The psi key is F until you rebind it. The menu section is Psi-Doom.

Co-op has not been tested.

Doom 1 has no super shotgun, and this mod does not add one. Astral drop is simply absent there. The other rigs are on the guns those maps already use.

## Briefing

Listen up, marines.

UAC Advanced Weapons R&D and UAC Psi-Corps have issued a new set of attachments for your arsenal. Every standard weapon except the sidearm now carries a psi-rig. That rig lets you drive an astral projection. Call it a soul, if a marine still has one.

Lab work is finished. Field work is not. There is no tactical manual. Nobody has taken these things into a fight and lived long enough to write one.

That is your task today. You will carry them into battle. You will watch what they do when the shooting starts. You will write it down: how they behave, and how a marine should use them. You are the only veterans we can send. You might even come back.

Bring every scrap. These rigs may turn the war against the demon hordes. A few million recruits are waiting on the manual you write with your own hide. What you see, what you try, and what gets you killed goes in the report.

Report at 1400 hours. Phobos teleport station.

Dismissed.

## Standing orders

One key runs every rig. It is F until you rebind it. Press with a weapon out and that weapon's rig comes on. Press again and it cuts, even if you have already swapped guns. The press after that uses whatever is in your hands. One rig at a time. You cannot stack them.

The weapons still shoot. The rig is the other control. Astral float is the exception: while it is up you are held to a walk, and the weapon stays cold, including after you put the shotgun away.

The fist, the chainsaw, and the sidearm were left alone. Knee-Deep has no super shotgun, so it has no astral drop.

Nothing new was placed on the maps, and nothing new was drawn. The rigs are on the guns you already know. The astral body is invisible. You will not see it. You learn it from where the fire goes.

A demon in arm's reach, a closet that opens on you, and a crusher still have the body. The card says when the shell is empty. An invulnerability sphere hauls the soul back into the body until the sphere fades, and the key does nothing while that lasts.

Some rigs spend ammunition. A miss can still spend it. The lab did not print the prices. Find them.

## Lab card

This is what the lab wrote down. It is not a field manual. Caution: your astral body can still be hit.

| Weapon | Attachment | Behaviour | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shotgun | Astral float | Project your astral body above you. | Confuses the enemy and throws their aim off. The astral body can still be hit. |
| Super shotgun | Astral drop | Leave your soul in place and push on as a hollow shell. | The shell does not take the hits. The soul does. It will not last long. Leave that soul somewhere safe. If it is destroyed while the shell is still fighting, you die. |
| Chaingun | Astral projection | Project your astral body out to the side. | Confuses the enemy and throws their aim off. The lab calls this a partial invulnerability. Extreme caution: the projection passes through walls, and it can still be hit. |
| Rocket launcher | Partial astral possession | Seize a monster in your sights and turn it on its own. | Starts an infight. Other demons attack that body at once. Caution: you can be hurt while you are in it. |
| Plasma rifle | Full astral possession | Take the monster over. Look it over. Then attack. | The infight starts when you attack, not before. Time is a luxury. Our best scientists are still working to make the hold last longer. Best use: possess, evaluate, attack, and return at once. You can still be hurt. |
| BFG 9000 | In the Mountains of Madness | Push demons clean out of their minds. | Havoc, and infights that spread. Use it with care, and spend it on the tougher monsters. Setting a whole pack running amok wastes a lot of energy. |
